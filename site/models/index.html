Local AI models FLE Link runs in one command

FLE Link reads your machine, installs the engine that fits and downloads the model. These are the models of the built-in catalog and recipes; any other GGUF file works with flelink run /path/to/model.gguf.

  • DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 763B mixture of experts, 232 GiB; 763B, hot half on the GPU
  • Qwen3.8-Flash-Next 125B mixture of experts, 88.9 GB
  • Katy 27B dense 27B with a draft model
  • Llama 3.2 1B 1B, a first try on any laptop

curl -fsSL https://flelink.run/install.sh | sh
