Run Katy 27B locally on a 12 to 32 GB GPU

Katy is a quantization of Swift-1.5-Qwen3.8-27B, a dense 27B model, as IQ4_XS with the DFlash2 draft model. From 20 GB of GPU memory all 66 layers are on the card; below that the rest runs on the CPU, which is slower.

flelink run katy-27b-dflash

What you need

One NVIDIA card from 12 GB and 24 GB of RAM. A 12 GB card is the minimum the recipe was measured with.

"no engine": this recipe needs the FLE engine, which has an NVIDIA CUDA build only. File size 16 GiB including the draft model.

The verdicts are the rule FLE Link applies before it downloads anything (the rules in full). A speed is shown only for the hardware it was measured on. A size nobody measured yet? flelink bench --task pagoda --share posts yours to the benchmarks board.

Good to know

  • Below 20 GB the speed depends on the CPU and the RAM: the numbers were measured on a Ryzen 9 9950X3D with 128 GB RAM, and a slower machine will be slower.
